FAQ Common questions

Before you ask.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

An engineer reviews it and contacts you to arrange an on-site load survey. We record your connected loads, peak demand and any existing generator, then prepare a detailed engineering proposal.

Which areas do you cover?

Our installation partner, Green Solar System, covers Lagos and Southwest Nigeria. If your site is elsewhere, tell us in your enquiry and we'll discuss what's possible.

How is pricing worked out?

After the survey we deliver a building-by-building bill of quantities, single-line diagrams, an equipment schedule and firm pricing. We don't quote final numbers without seeing the site.

Can the system work with my existing diesel generator?

Yes. Microgrid systems integrate the generator directly and start it automatically during extended outages. Hybrid systems connect it through an automatic transfer switch.

Can we start small and expand later?

Yes. Projects can be phased, starting with your most critical loads, and each phase can be funded independently. Our hybrid inverters support a DC/AC ratio of up to 1.5, leaving room to add more panels.

How long do the batteries last?

Our cabinets use LFP (lithium iron phosphate) chemistry rated for 6,000+ cycles, with liquid cooling to protect the cells in hot climates.

Who maintains the system after installation?

Green Solar System provides local after-sales service. Systems include cloud monitoring with live status, daily generation and fault alarms, so issues are spotted early.
