05 How we choose equipment

Two tiers, not one-size-fits-all.

A single inverter type for a whole site is either too expensive for the classrooms or not safe enough for the hospital. So we split the site by how much interruption each load can tolerate.

Hybrid inverter + YCDT cabinet

Teaching buildings, offices, estates, hotel rooms. Lower capital cost, a proven ecosystem and easy local maintenance.

  • 10 MPPT

Microgrid PCS + YCDT cabinet

Labs, theatres, patient monitoring, cold-chain medicines. Zero interruption, black start and seamless generator integration.
