04 Our approach

Not every load can wait two seconds.

Most sites mix loads that can ride through a short switchover with loads that cannot. We size the hardware to the load instead of selling one inverter for everything, so you don't overpay for classrooms or under-protect a hospital.

How two-tier design works

Everyday loads

Lights, fans, offices, classrooms. Served by hybrid inverters and storage cabinets.

Seconds of switchover is fine

Sensitive loads

Lab instruments, fridges, process control. A brief interruption ruins samples and data.

Needs seamless power

Critical loads

Patient monitoring, theatres, cold-chain medicines. Served by a microgrid PCS.

Under 20 ms · zero downtime
