01 Who we are

A storage integrator, with engineers on the ground.

YCDT Solar is an energy storage system integrator based in Hangzhou, China. We specialise in liquid-cooled battery cabinets, hybrid inverter systems and complete commercial and industrial solar-plus-storage solutions.

Our systems are built for markets where outages are long, voltage is unstable and diesel is expensive: Africa, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.

In Nigeria we work hand in hand with Green Solar System, a Nigerian-registered engineering company that surveys, installs, commissions and services every system we deliver. You get factory-direct hardware with local accountability.

03 How we work

Four things we won't compromise on.

01

Engineered for weak grids

Long outages, voltage swings and heat are the design brief, not an edge case. That's why our cabinets are liquid-cooled and outdoor-rated.

02

Right-sized, not oversold

We match the hardware to each load. UPS-grade equipment where it matters, economical hybrids everywhere else.

03

Local service for life

We choose platforms with parts and know-how available locally, so maintenance is realistic for years, not months.

04

Numbers you can check

We survey before we quote. You get a building-by-building bill of quantities and firm pricing, not a brochure estimate.
