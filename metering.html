Home/Metering

Smart metering, powered by Smeter.

Prepaid smart meters for estates, apartments and shared solar systems, so every unit pays for exactly what it uses. Tenants buy power from their phone; owners get paid automatically.

Visit smeter.app Plan metering for your site
01 Who it's for

One meter, three people it works for.

Tenants

Buy power from the Smeter app in seconds, check your balance and usage, and get alerts before you run low. No scratch cards, no queues.

Landlords & estates

Every purchase is settled to you automatically, with usage and payments for each meter in one dashboard.

Solar homes & shared systems

Visibility of your power use, and a fair way to sell power from a shared solar + storage plant to each home on an estate.

02 How it works

From install to paid automatically.

1

We install the meters

One smart meter per unit, wired into your distribution and commissioned by our engineers.

2

Tenants buy in the app

Each tenant signs in with their meter and pays from their phone.

3

Power is credited

The token goes to the meter over the air. Where there's no signal, it can be entered manually.

4

Owners are settled

Payments and usage reports land with the property owner, with no cash handling.

03 Why Smeter

A prepaid meter, rebuilt around the phone.

Smeter moves the screen and keypad of a traditional prepaid meter into a mobile app. The parts most likely to break or be tampered with simply aren't there, and tenants control everything from a device they already carry.

The product For property owners
  • Screenless design: no LCD or keypad to fail or tamper with
  • Mobile app for recharge, balance, history and alerts
  • Cellular (NB-IoT/GSM) cloud sync and Bluetooth for local setup
  • Automatic revenue settlement for landlords
  • Tokens delivered over the air, or entered manually without signal
04 Better together

Meter the power you generate.

Metering turns a shared solar + storage plant into something an estate can run fairly. We design the generation, storage, automation and metering as one system.

Shared storage

A central battery system for the whole estate.

Automatic changeover

Grid, solar and generator switched without anyone on site.

Per-unit billing

Each home pays for its own share through Smeter.
